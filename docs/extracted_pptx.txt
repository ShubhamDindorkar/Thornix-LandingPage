The hardest place on earth to keep a well honest
GCC oil & gas fields run thousands of wells through conditions that break ordinary monitoring systems.
Shamal dust storms
40–60+ km/h winds, sub-200 m visibility — sensors degrade, comms drop.
Extreme heat & sour gas
50 °C heat kills gas detectors; high H₂S means errors cost lives.
Sand & water
Sand erodes equipment; water and gas come up mixed with the oil.
Sensors that lie
30–50% of sensors can be suspect at once — most 'alarms' are bad data, not bad wells.
